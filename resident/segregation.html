Practical Waste Management Tips for Households
Rinse Recyclables

Rinse plastic bottles, soda cans, and jars before throwing them into your recyclables bin to prevent foul smells and pests.

Compost Organic Waste

Vegetable and fruit peels can be placed into garden compost pits instead of garbage bags to nourish household plants.

Separate Hazardous Items

Never combine broken mercury bulbs or leaking batteries with regular trash. Save them for the monthly hazardous waste collection.

Flatten Cardboard Boxes

Break down and fold bulky carton and cereal boxes to maximize garbage storage space and make transport easier.
